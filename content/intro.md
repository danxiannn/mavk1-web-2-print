Time is everywhere, yet it remains invisible. We measure it through the movement of hands, the shifting of shadows, the phases of the Moon and the changing positions of stars.

Across different periods and cultures, people have invented instruments, drawn maps, built calendars and imagined cosmic systems to make sense of its passage.

This collection brings together these objects, images and ideas — from clocks and astronomical instruments to celestial maps, calendars and mythology...exploring the many ways in which time has been measured, represented and imagined.
